About this dashboard

Field data were collected by manual observation at three points along the one-way corridor from the Davao–Cotabato National Highway to the University of Southern Mindanao (USM) main gate: classified vehicle counts (entry), friction events and tricycle dwell times (mid-segment), and arrival and pedestrian counts (USM gate), for the morning (06:30–07:30), midday (11:15–12:15) and evening (19:30–20:30) peaks.

The SUMO model inserts the counted vehicles by class and interval, reproduces the observed tricycle stops, parking and side-street intrusions and pedestrian crossings, and compares five scenarios: S0 existing conditions, S1 tricycle loading bay, S2 no-parking enforcement, S3 controlled pedestrian crossing, and S4 all three combined.

BSCE Highway & Railway Engineering, Department of Civil Engineering, University of Southern Mindanao, Kabacan, Cotabato.

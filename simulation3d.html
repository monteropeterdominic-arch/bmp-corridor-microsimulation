How to use the 3D simulation

  1. The traffic starts playing by itself. Use ❚❚ Pause / ▶ Play to stop and restart it, and speed to watch faster.
  2. USM Avenue is one-way with two lanes in the same direction (dashed line), from the Davao–Cotabato Road to the USM gate.
  3. Switch S0 Existing ↔ S4 Combined measures to compare the queue at the USM gate.
  4. Use the view buttons (Overview, Street level, Top-down), or drag to rotate, right-drag to tilt and scroll to zoom.
  5. Drag the time slider to jump to any moment between 07:00 and 07:15.
  6. ⛶ Full screen fills the screen; press Esc to leave.
Keyboard: Space play/pause · F full screen · 1/2/3 views
